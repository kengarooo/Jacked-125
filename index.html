How we adjust

  • Protein stays high to support muscle retention/growth.
  • We use weekly averages and waist trends, not single-day scale changes.
  • If weight/waist stall for several weeks, calories may be adjusted modestly.
  • If performance, recovery or hunger deteriorate, calories may need to come up.
  • The goal is a lean, muscular physique—not simply the lowest scale number.
